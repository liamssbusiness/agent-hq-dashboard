# Palmier Edit System — Hand Me Footage, I Help You Grow

This is the collaboration protocol between **you** (films + opens Palmier) and
**me, Claude** (operates the timeline over MCP, clarifies your goal, optimizes
for growth). Read once, then just use the **Footage Brief** every time.

> **How it physically works:** You film → open the project in Palmier on your Mac
> (MCP server live) → connect me (Claude Desktop/Code) → paste a filled-in
> **Footage Brief** → I ask a few sharp questions, then drive the cuts. I can't
> *watch* your video, so the Brief + you scrubbing and describing clips is how I
> "see" it. The better the Brief, the better the edit.

---

## The loop (5 phases)

```
1. BRIEF      → you fill the template, paste it to me
2. CLARIFY    → I ask ≤5 questions to lock the goal & hook
3. EDIT PLAN  → I propose the cut structure; you approve/tweak
4. EXECUTE    → I drive Palmier (trim, reorder, captions, B-roll)
5. OPTIMIZE   → we grade the result vs growth checklist, then export
```

---

## Phase 1 — The Footage Brief (copy this every time)

```
=== FOOTAGE BRIEF ===
PLATFORM:        (TikTok / Shorts / YouTube long / multiple)
WORKING TITLE:   (one line — what's this video?)
MY ORIGINAL IDEA: (2–3 sentences: what I set out to film and why)
TARGET LENGTH:   (e.g. 30s / 60s / 8min)
THE ONE TAKEAWAY: (the single thing a viewer remembers)
WHO IT'S FOR:    (who scrolls and thinks "this is for me")
CALL TO ACTION:  (follow / comment / watch next / none)

CLIPS I HAVE (rough list — scrub Palmier and describe each):
  1. (00:00–00:xx) what happens
  2. ...
  3. ...
HOOK OPTIONS I FILMED: (which clips could open the video)
B-ROLL I HAVE:        (cutaway shots available)
MUSIC/VIBE:           (energy: hype / calm / funny / serious)
WHAT I'M UNSURE ABOUT: (where you want my help most)
=== END BRIEF ===
```

You don't need to fill every field perfectly — even half of it gives me enough
to start asking the right questions.

---

## Phase 2 — How I'll clarify your goal (my job)

I'll ask at most ~5 questions, aimed at the things that actually move growth:

- **Hook test:** "In one sentence, why does someone NOT scroll past the first 2 seconds?"
- **Promise/payoff:** "What did you promise in the hook, and where in the footage do you deliver it?"
- **Audience fit:** "Is this for people who already know you, or strangers? (changes how much context we keep)"
- **Single focus:** "If we had to cut this to ONE point, which clip is non-negotiable?"
- **Tone:** "Fast & punchy, or slow & intentional?"

If your original idea and your best footage point in different directions, I'll
say so plainly and recommend the stronger angle — that's the most valuable part
of this process.

---

## Phase 3 — Edit plan (I propose, you approve)

I'll hand you a structure before touching the timeline, e.g.:

```
HOOK (0–2s):     clip 4, your best take of "the result"
SETUP (2–8s):    clip 1, trimmed — just enough context
PAYOFF (8–45s):  clips 2→5→3, fast cuts, silences removed
CTA (45–50s):    clip 6 + caption
B-ROLL:          generate 3s cutaway at the "X" mention
CAPTIONS:        full, burned in
```

You say "yes" or "swap X for Y," then I execute.

---

## Phase 4 — What I'll tell Palmier to do (executed via MCP)

Standard moves I run for you:
- Remove silences, filler, and retakes — keep the best take of each line
- Put the strongest hook first
- Tighten pacing to your target length
- Add burned-in captions
- Generate B-roll **only** where you couldn't film it (this is the only step that spends credits)
- Match cuts to the music beat where it helps

You watch the result, give notes ("too slow at 0:10", "wrong hook"), I revise.

---

## Phase 5 — Growth optimization checklist (we grade before export)

A video doesn't ship until it passes this:

| Check | Pass condition |
|-------|----------------|
| **Hook** | First 2 seconds make a stranger stop scrolling |
| **Promise kept** | The hook's implied promise is paid off, fast |
| **No dead air** | Zero boring gaps; every second earns its place |
| **One idea** | The video is about ONE thing, not three |
| **Captions** | On, readable on mute |
| **Watch-on-mute test** | Still makes sense with sound off |
| **Loop / re-watch** | Ending invites a re-watch or the next video |
| **Clear CTA** | Viewer knows what to do next (if any) |
| **Right aspect** | 9:16 vertical for TikTok/Shorts, 16:9 for long YT |
| **Thumbnail/cover** | (YouTube) a frame/cover that earns the click |

Anything that fails, we fix before exporting.

## Repurpose for free reach (one shoot → many posts)
After the main cut, ask me:
- *"Pull 2–3 standalone 15s clips from this for TikTok/Shorts."*
- *"Make a horizontal version for YouTube."*
- *"Suggest 3 alt hooks so I can A/B test which opener works."*

One filming session should become several posts.

---

## Growth flywheel (the part that compounds)
1. **Post consistently** — a steady schedule beats perfect-but-rare.
2. **Watch retention** — where do viewers drop off? Tell me the timestamp; we cut harder there next time.
3. **Double down on winners** — when a video pops, we save it as a **template** and make more in that format.
4. **Iterate the hook** — most growth lives in the first 2 seconds; we keep testing openers.
5. **Build a back-catalog** — each video should point to the next (CTA / series).
