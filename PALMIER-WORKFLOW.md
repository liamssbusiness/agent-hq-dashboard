# Palmier — Cost, Learning Curve & a YouTube/TikTok System

This is the "what will it actually cost me and how do I get good" companion to
`PALMIER-SETUP.md`. No exact video ideas here — this is the **repeatable system**
you run for every video.

---

## 💰 What editing will actually cost

There are **two separate cost buckets**. Don't confuse them.

### Bucket 1 — The editor itself: **$0**
Trimming, cutting, reordering, captions, exporting, and Claude *operating the
timeline* are **free**. If you film your own footage and just edit it, you can
make videos for **nothing**. The open-source editor + MCP control cost zero.

### Bucket 2 — AI generation: pay-as-you-go credits
You only spend money when you **generate new media** (AI B-roll, AI images,
AI clips) inside the timeline. During **early access it's free**; expect paid
credits as it matures.

**Rough credit math (their stated guide):**
> ~5,000 credits ≈ **333 images** *or* **3–7 minutes** of generated video.

Cost depends on the model + resolution you pick. For reference, the underlying
models priced elsewhere run roughly:
- **Seedance** ≈ $0.10–$0.80 per minute of generated video
- **Kling** ≈ ~$0.03 per second (~$1.80/min)

### Realistic monthly estimate for a creator
| Your style | AI generation use | Likely cost |
|------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Film everything yourself, edit only** | None | **$0** |
| Mostly your footage + a little AI B-roll | ~1–2 AI min/video, ~10 vids/mo | **low — a few $/mo** once paid plans land |
| Heavy AI-generated content | many minutes of AI video/video | this is where it adds up — budget per the per-minute rates above |

👉 **Your cheapest, highest-quality path: film real footage, use Palmier + Claude
to edit it fast, and sprinkle AI generation only for B-roll/transitions you
can't film.** That keeps you at ~$0 or near it.

## ⏱️ How long to actually get good

Honest expectations, because Palmier is two skills at once: *video editing* +
*directing an AI agent*.

| Stage | Time | What "good" looks like |
|-------|------|------------------------|
| **Productive day 1** | 1–2 hrs | Import clips, cut, trim, add captions, export a vertical video |
| **Comfortable** | ~1 week (3–4 short edits) | You stop thinking about the buttons; Claude does the boring cuts |
| **Genuinely good / fast** | 3–6 weeks of consistent posting | Tight pacing, strong hooks, a look that's recognizably *yours* |
| **"I have a machine"** | ~2–3 months | A repeatable template; each video takes far less time than your first |

The AI is a huge accelerant — the editing-mechanics learning curve that
normally takes months gets compressed because Claude handles the tedious parts.
Your real skill investment is **storytelling, pacing, and hooks**, not software.

**Biggest time-saver:** reuse a template. Once a video format works, save it and
tell Claude *"edit this the same way as last time."*

## 🎬 The system: film → edit → publish (run this every time)

A repeatable loop so you're never staring at a blank timeline.

### Step 1 — Plan (5 min, before filming)
Decide three things and write them in one line:
- **Hook** (first 2 seconds — the reason they don't scroll)
- **Payoff** (the one thing they'll remember)
- **Format** (talking-head? voiceover + B-roll? tutorial?)

### Step 2 — Film (capture more than you need)
- Shoot **vertical 9:16** for TikTok/Shorts; **16:9** for main YouTube.
- Film your hook **2–3 different ways** — you'll pick the best in editing.
- Leave a beat of silence at the start/end of each take (easier to cut).
- Get 2–3 short "B-roll" shots (hands, surroundings, the thing you're talking about).

### Step 3 — Dump & rough cut (Claude does the heavy lifting)
Open the project in Palmier, import everything, then tell Claude:
1. *"Remove all the silent gaps and my retakes — keep the best take of each line."*
2. *"Put the strongest hook clip first."*
3. *"Tighten it to under [60s for TikTok / your target] and keep the pacing fast."*

### Step 4 — Polish (you + Claude)
- *"Add captions for the whole thing."* (captions = huge for retention on mute)
- *"Generate a 3-second B-roll of [X] and place it where I mention it"* — only here do you spend credits, and only if you couldn't film it.
- Add your music/sound. Watch once on mute — does the hook still land?

### Step 5 — Export per platform
- **TikTok / Reels / Shorts:** 9:16, ≤ 60s (or 90s), captions burned in.
- **YouTube long-form:** 16:9, with chapters if it's a tutorial.
- Export both a vertical *and* a horizontal cut when it makes sense — one shoot, two platforms.

### Step 6 — Build your template (the compounding move)
After a video performs well:
- Save the project as a **template**.
- Note what the hook/pacing was.
- Next time: *"Edit this like my [template name] video."*
Your edit time drops every cycle.

## 📌 Platform cheat-sheet

| | TikTok | YouTube Shorts | YouTube long-form |
|---|--------|----------------|-------------------|
| Aspect | 9:16 | 9:16 | 16:9 |
| Length sweet spot | 15–60s | 15–60s | 6–12 min |
| First-2-sec hook | critical | critical | important |
| Captions | yes | yes | optional |
| One shoot → | export vertical | same vertical export | film/repurpose horizontally |

## TL;DR
- **Editing your own footage = free.** You only pay to *generate* AI media, and it's free during early access.
- **Day-1 productive, ~1 month to good, ~2–3 months to fast & polished** — Claude shortcuts most of the software learning.
- **Run the 6-step loop every video**, film vertical, lead with a hook, let Claude do the boring cuts, save templates so each one gets faster.
