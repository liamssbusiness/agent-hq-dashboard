# Palmier — Part 2 Cheat Card (copy-paste phrases)

Phone-friendly. During the edit, just paste these to me in order. No typing on a
tiny screen, no jargon to remember. Skip any that don't apply.

---

### 0. Kick off
> "Part 1 done. Palmier is open with my clips. Walk me through Part 2 step by step — I've never edited like this before."

### 1. Connect Claude (I'll guide the click)
> "Help me connect Claude to Palmier now."

### 2. Let me 'see' the footage
> "Clips are imported. I'll describe each one — ready."
*(then scrub each clip and tell me what's in it)*

### 3. Pick the hook
> "Compare my two result takes and tell me which should open the video, and why."

### 4. Rough cut
> "Remove the mess-up, all the pauses, and the dead air around my outro line. Keep the best take of everything."

### 5. Structure & pace
> "Put the hook first, order the steps, and tighten the whole thing to about 20–25 seconds. Make the cuts snappy."

### 6. Polish (the 'looks expensive' pass)
> "Add burned-in captions for the whole video."
> "Drop my hands close-up as a cutaway over one of the steps."
> "Generate one 3-second B-roll cutaway of [thing] and place it where it fits."
> "Add light upbeat music and sync the cuts to the beat."

### 7. Fix notes (use as needed while watching)
> "It's too slow at [time] — cut harder there."
> "Wrong hook — try the other take."
> "Captions are too big/small — adjust."
> "Make the ending tighter."

### 8. Quality check
> "Run it against the post-ready checklist and tell me anything that fails."

### 9. Export & repurpose
> "Export it as a 9:16 vertical file ready to post."
> "Now pull 2 standalone 15-second clips from this for extra posts."
> "Save this as a template so my next video is faster."
