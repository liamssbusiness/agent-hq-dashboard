# Palmier — Practice Video List + First Tutorial Edit

Goal of this phase: **learn the Claude-in-Palmier loop** with throwaway footage.
The content doesn't matter. We're testing the *process* so it's effortless later.

---

## The list — easy practice videos (in order)

Each one deliberately exercises one new editing skill. Do them in order.

| # | Video | ~Film time | Skill it teaches |
|---|-------|-----------|------------------|
| **1** | **"30-second coffee" (or any quick task you do)** | 5 min | The full loop: import → cut silences/retakes → hook-first → captions → export. **Start here.** |
| 2 | "3 things on my desk" | 5 min | Reordering clips, picking the strongest opener |

After #6 you'll know every core move. Then we go real.

> Everything below is the full plan for **#1**. Don't worry about 2–6 yet.

## VIDEO #1 — "The 30-Second [Task]" tutorial edit

Pick the most boring 30-second task you do: making coffee, tying your shoes,
booting your PC, pouring cereal. **Boring is the point** — we want clean footage
to learn on, not a masterpiece.

### What to film (the shot list)

Film these as **separate clips, vertical 9:16**, phone is fine. Total ~5 min:

1. **The "result" shot (your hook):** the finished thing. The full cup of coffee,
   the tied shoe. Film this **twice** so we can practice picking the best take.
2. **Step 1 clip:** the start of the task (grab the mug / the laces).
3. **Step 2 clip:** the middle. **On purpose: mess up once, pause, then redo it.**
   This gives us a retake + a silent gap to practice cutting out.
4. **Step 3 clip:** the finish.
5. **One cutaway:** a close-up of your hands or the object. (B-roll practice.)
6. **One line to camera:** say *"…and that's how you do it"* — leave 2 seconds of
   silence before and after. (Practice trimming dead air around a take.)

That's it. Don't reshoot for quality. Messy is good here.

### What I'll do once you paste it (so you know the plan)

1. **Confirm the hook** — pick the better "result" take (1 vs 2) to open with.
2. **Propose this structure** before cutting:
   ```
   HOOK (0–2s):   result shot (best take)
   STEPS (2–18s): step1 → step2 (mess-up removed) → step3, silences cut
   CUTAWAY:       hands clip dropped over a step
   OUTRO (18–22s): "that's how you do it", trimmed tight
   CAPTIONS:      burned in, full
   ```
3. **Drive Palmier via MCP** to:
   - delete the mess-up + pauses
   - order clips hook-first
   - trim the dead air around the outro line
   - add captions
   - tighten to ~22s
4. **Optional B-roll test:** I'll generate one 3s AI cutaway so you see how that
   step works (free during early access). We'll only do it once, to learn it.
5. **Grade it** against the growth checklist, then export.

### The exact thing you'll paste to kick it off

> Walk me through it step by step — I've never done this before."
