# PART 1 — Download & Film (do this solo)

### Step 1 — Download Palmier (on good wifi)
- [ ] Confirm your Mac: Apple menu → *About This Mac* → needs **Apple Silicon (M1+)** and **macOS 26 Tahoe**. If not, tell me before buying/updating anything.
- [ ] Go to **github.com/palmier-io/palmier-pro** → **Releases** → download the latest `.dmg`.
- [ ] Drag Palmier Pro into Applications. First open: right-click → *Open* → *Open*.
- [ ] Launch it once to confirm it runs. (You can skip login for now — not needed to film/import.)
- [ ] *(We'll connect Claude together in Part 2 — don't worry about MCP yet.)*

### Step 2 — Film the practice clips
Pick the **most boring 30-second task you do** (make coffee, tie a shoe, pour
cereal). Boring = clean footage to learn on. Shoot **vertical (9:16)** on your phone.

Film these as **separate clips** (~5 min total):
- [ ] **Result shot ×2** — the finished thing, filmed twice (two takes)
- [ ] **Step 1** — the start of the task
- [ ] **Step 2** — the middle, and *on purpose:* **mess up, pause, then redo it**
- [ ] **Step 3** — the finish
- [ ] **Hands close-up** — one cutaway shot
- [ ] **One line to camera:** say *"…and that's how you do it"* with **2 seconds of silence before and after**

Rules: don't reshoot for quality, don't delete the mess-up — the imperfections
are what we practice fixing.

### Step 3 — Get it onto the Mac
- [ ] AirDrop / upload the clips to the Mac with Palmier on it.
- [ ] (Optional) Open Palmier and drag the clips in so they're ready.

### ✅ Part 1 is done when:
You've installed Palmier, filmed all 7 clips, and they're on your Mac.

**→ Then message me: "Part 1 done"** and we begin Part 2.

# PART 2 — Edit to Post-Ready (we do this together)

> Preview so you know what's coming. We do this live — you'll be at the Mac,
> Palmier open, and I'll drive the timeline through MCP while talking you through it.

### What we'll do, in order
1. **Connect Claude** — I walk you through Help → MCP Instructions → Install in Claude Desktop (one click). ~2 min.
2. **Import & label** — get all clips on the timeline; you scrub and describe each so I "see" them.
3. **Lock the hook** — we pick the better result-take to open with (the scroll-stopper).
4. **Rough cut** — I remove the mess-up, the pauses, and the dead air around your outro line.
5. **Structure & pace** — order it hook-first, tighten to ~20–25s, snappy cuts.
6. **Polish for "looks expensive":**
   - burned-in **captions** (huge for retention)
   - one **AI-generated B-roll** cutaway so it feels produced (free in early access)
   - **match-cut** the hands close-up over a step
   - light **music** synced so cuts land on the beat
   - clean color/levels pass if needed
7. **Quality grade** — we run the post-ready checklist (below). Anything failing, we fix.
8. **Export** — vertical file ready to post. Optional: pull 2 extra short clips from it.

### The "looks like tons of work" levers (what actually makes it feel pro)
- **Tight pacing** — no dead second; this alone separates amateur from polished
- **Captions** styled and on-beat
- **One B-roll cutaway** at the right moment — hides cuts, adds production value
- **Music synced to cuts** — your brain reads this as "high effort"
- **Strong 2-second hook** — the difference between watched and scrolled-past

### Post-ready checklist (must all pass before export)
- [ ] First 2 seconds stop the scroll
- [ ] Zero dead air / no rambling
- [ ] One clear idea, start to finish
- [ ] Captions on and readable on mute
- [ ] Makes sense with sound off
- [ ] Cuts feel intentional (and hit the music)
- [ ] 9:16 vertical, ~20–25s
- [ ] Ending invites a re-watch
